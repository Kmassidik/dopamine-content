# Curriculum roadmap

Generated from `content/catalog.json` and the lesson files. Every lesson has reading pages (a worked example included) before its practice questions, every unit has a cheat sheet, and the app builds a unit quiz from each unit. See STANDARD.md for the rules each course follows.

Order of study inside a track is the course order below.

## Math (traditional sequence: Pre-Algebra, Algebra 1, Geometry, Algebra 2, Precalculus)

### 1. Pre-Algebra  (23 lessons, about 120 min)
_Work with negatives, fractions, decimals, percents and ratios, then take your first steps with variables, equations and inequalities._

- **Integers and Operations** (6): Negative numbers and the number line, Adding and subtracting integers, Multiplying and dividing integers, Exponents and powers, Order of operations, Factors, multiples and primes
- **Fractions, Decimals and Percents** (6): Fractions and equivalent fractions, Adding and subtracting fractions, Multiplying and dividing fractions, Decimals and rounding, Percents, Percent applications
- **Ratios, Rates and Proportions** (5): Ratios, Unit rates, Proportions, Scale drawings and maps, Percent change
- **Expressions and Equations** (6): Variables and expressions, Evaluating and simplifying, The distributive property, One-step equations, Two-step equations, An introduction to inequalities

### 2. Algebra 1  (28 lessons, about 160 min)
_Build algebra from the ground up: equations, lines and functions, inequalities and systems, exponents and polynomials, and quadratics. Short lessons with worked examples and practice._

- **Linear Equations** (6): One-step equations, Two-step equations, Variables on both sides, Brackets and fractions, Formulas and literal equations, Word problems with equations
- **Linear Functions and Graphs** (6): Relations and functions, Slope, Slope-intercept form, Graphing lines, Point-slope and standard form, Parallel and perpendicular lines
- **Inequalities and Systems** (5): Solving inequalities, Compound and absolute value, Systems by graphing, Systems by substitution, Systems by elimination
- **Exponents and Polynomials** (6): Exponent rules, Zero and negative exponents, Scientific notation, Adding and subtracting polynomials, Multiplying polynomials, Factoring polynomials
- **Quadratics** (5): Parabolas and quadratic functions, Solving by factoring, Square roots and completing the square, The quadratic formula, The discriminant and applications

### 3. Geometry  (28 lessons, about 150 min)
_Learn how shapes work, from points and angles to triangles, circles and solids. Each lesson is a short reading followed by practice, and every result comes with a reason._

- **Points, Lines and Angles** (6): Points, lines and planes, Measuring angles, Angle pairs, Parallel lines and transversals, Distance and midpoint, Reasoning and simple proofs
- **Triangles** (6): Classifying triangles, Angle sum and exterior angles, Congruent triangles, Isosceles and equilateral triangles, The Pythagorean theorem, Special right triangles
- **Similarity and Right-Triangle Trig** (5): Similar figures and scale factor, Proving similarity, Proportional parts, Sine, cosine and tangent, Solving right triangles
- **Polygons, Circles and Area** (6): Quadrilaterals, Angles of polygons, Perimeter and area, Circles: circumference and area, Arcs, chords and inscribed angles, Sector area and arc length
- **Solids and Transformations** (5): Prisms and cylinders, Pyramids, cones and spheres, Surface area, Reflections, rotations, translations, Dilations and symmetry

### 4. Algebra 2  (22 lessons, about 120 min)
_Go beyond Algebra 1 with functions, polynomials, complex numbers, exponentials, logarithms, sequences and probability. Short readings, then practice._

- **Functions and Transformations** (5): Function notation, domain and range, Composition and inverse functions, Transformations of graphs, Piecewise and absolute value functions, Radicals and rational exponents
- **Polynomials and Complex Numbers** (6): Polynomial arithmetic and long division, The remainder and factor theorems, Zeros and the rational root theorem, Complex numbers, Quadratics in vertex form, Solving polynomial equations
- **Exponentials and Logarithms** (5): Exponential growth and decay, Compound interest and the number e, Logarithms, Properties of logarithms, Solving exponential and log equations
- **Rational Functions and Sequences** (6): Rational expressions, Rational equations, Arithmetic sequences, Geometric sequences, Series and sigma notation, Probability basics and the normal curve

### 5. Precalculus  (22 lessons, about 120 min)
_Build the toolkit for calculus: function families, trigonometry, applications of trig and the first ideas of limits and derivatives. Short readings, then practice._

- **Function Families** (5): Polynomial end behavior and zeros, Rational functions and asymptotes, Exponential and logarithmic models, Inverse functions revisited, Modeling with functions
- **Trigonometry** (7): Radians and angle measure, The unit circle, Graphs of sine and cosine, Tangent and other trig graphs, Inverse trig functions, Trig identities, Solving trig equations
- **Applications of Trigonometry** (5): The law of sines, The law of cosines, Vectors as arrows, Polar coordinates, Complex numbers in polar form
- **Toward Calculus** (5): Conic sections, Sequences and series, The idea of a limit, Rates of change, The derivative idea

## Programming (the C track follows the weekly order of Harvard's CS50x: C, arrays, algorithms, memory, data structures; then a short Python course)

### 1. C Fundamentals  (23 lessons, about 150 min)
_Write, compile and run your first C programs. Learn variables, types, conditions, loops and functions, then solve small problems step by step._

- **Your First Programs** (5): What programming is, Hello, world and main, Compiling and running, printf and escape sequences, Comments, pseudocode, errors
- **Variables, Types and Operators** (6): Variables and int, char, float and double, printf and scanf formats, Arithmetic operators, Integer division and modulo, Overflow and float surprises
- **Conditions and Loops** (6): if and else, Relational and logical operators, else if and switch, while loops, for loops, Nested loops and do while
- **Functions and Problem Solving** (6): Defining and calling functions, Arguments and return values, Prototypes and scope, Constants and macros, Breaking a problem into steps, Mini challenges

### 2. Arrays, Strings and Debugging  (21 lessons, about 130 min)
_See how C source becomes a program, learn to find bugs with warnings, prints, a debugger and asserts, then master arrays, strings and the command line._

- **How C Becomes a Program** (5): The preprocessor, The compiler, Assembling and linking, Headers and #include, Multiple files and make
- **Debugging** (5): Kinds of bugs, Compiler messages and warnings, printf debugging, Using a debugger, Assertions and testing
- **Arrays** (5): Declaring and indexing, Loops over arrays, Arrays and functions, Two-dimensional arrays, Going out of bounds
- **Strings and the Command Line** (6): Strings as char arrays, The string.h library, The ctype.h library, argc and argv, Reading input with fgets, Mini challenges

### 3. Algorithms in C  (21 lessons, about 110 min)
_Learn how to describe and compare algorithms with big O, then write searching, sorting and recursive algorithms in C and see why some are faster._

- **Thinking About Algorithms** (5): What an algorithm is, Counting steps, Big O notation, Omega and theta, Best and worst case
- **Searching** (5): Linear search, Binary search, Binary search in C, Comparing searches, Searching strings and structs
- **Sorting** (6): Bubble sort, Selection sort, Insertion sort, The idea of merge sort, Merging in C, Comparing sorts
- **Recursion** (5): The idea of recursion, Base and recursive cases, Factorial and Fibonacci, Recursion versus loops, Divide and conquer

### 4. Memory and Pointers  (22 lessons, about 110 min)
_Learn how memory really works in C: bytes, hex and addresses, pointers, malloc and free, structs and files, with every example tested._

- **Bytes, Hex and Addresses** (5): Bits and bytes, Hexadecimal, Memory as numbered bytes, The address-of operator, sizeof and types
- **Pointers** (6): Declaring pointers, Dereferencing, Pointers and arrays, Pointer arithmetic, Strings as char pointers, Pointers in functions, NULL
- **Dynamic Memory** (6): The stack and the heap, malloc, free, Memory leaks and Valgrind, realloc and growing arrays, Common memory bugs
- **Structs and Files** (5): Structs, typedef, fopen and fclose, Reading and writing text files, Bytes, binary files and images

### 5. Data Structures in C  (21 lessons, about 100 min)
_Build the classic data structures in C using pointers and malloc: stacks and queues, linked lists, hash tables, binary search trees and tries, and learn when to pick each._

- **Abstract Data Types** (4): What is an abstract data type?, Arrays and dynamic arrays, Stacks, Queues
- **Linked Lists** (6): Nodes, Building a list, Traversing a list, Inserting into a list, Deleting from a list, Trade-offs and doubly linked lists
- **Hash Tables** (5): The idea of hashing, Hash functions, Collisions, Separate chaining, Building a hash table in C
- **Trees and Tries** (6): Trees, Binary search trees, Searching and inserting, Tree traversals, Tries, Choosing a data structure

### 6. Programming Basics  (4 lessons, about 20 min)
_Learn to think in code with Python. Start with variables and types, then teach your programs to make decisions._

- **Variables and Types** (2): Variables, Numbers and text
- **Decisions** (2): If and else, Comparing values

## Science

- **Matter** (2): States of matter, Atoms and molecules
